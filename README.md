# Artificial Intelligence in Professional Football

## Overview

This repository contains an undergraduate research paper examining the influence of artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data-driven technologies on professional football.

The paper explores how technological advancements have transformed modern football through improvements in officiating, tactical analysis, player monitoring, and team evaluation. Particular attention is given to the ways in which artificial intelligence assists decision-making while maintaining human oversight within the sport.

## Abstract

Artificial intelligence (AI) is revolutionizing professional football, offering innovative solutions across various facets of the sport. Owing to geographic location, it is important to state for the duration of this paper, the American term “soccer” will be referred to as “football.” 

This paper explores the impact of AI in professional football, focusing on its applications first in officiating with the use of Goal Line Technology (GL), Video Assistant Referee (VAR) and Semi-Automated Offside Technology (SAOT). Then in analysis focusing on both tactical analysis and wearable technology. And finally, the ranking systems of both UEFA and FIFA competitions, and providing a brief suggestion as to where ranking systems utilizing AI could be headed. 

Future directions of AI could possibly include real-time tactical decision support systems, predictive models for injury prevention coordinating AI with psychological and historical data, and immersive training environments utilizing augmented and virtual reality.

By bridging technology with human expertise, AI reshapes football into a more dynamic, efficient domain. AI-driven systems enhance decision-making processes by utilizing machine learning models to analyze vast datasets. 

## Topics Covered
